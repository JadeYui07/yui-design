## Design Context

### Users
Hiring managers, design directors, and recruiters evaluating mid-to-senior UX/Product designers. They browse on desktop during work hours, skimming for evidence of strategic thinking, craft, and impact. They have seen hundreds of portfolios and are allergic to generic templates. They want to understand the designer's decision-making process, not just the output.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **precise, authoritative, human**. Yuzhuo Zhang solves hard product problems — life-safety apps, global design systems, complex EdTech workflows — with rigorous process and measurable outcomes. The tone is confident without being arrogant; the work speaks with evidence rather than hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark for case studies (moody, focused, appropriate to the content — weather radar, sleep tracking, design systems all benefit from concentrated viewing); light for the index/overview (bright, inviting first impression)
- **Visual tone**: Editorial / long-form journalism — think a design magazine feature, not a marketing page. Typography does the heavy lifting. Sections feel like chapters. White space is generous and intentional.
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS landing pages; the "big number + card grid" portfolio template; glassmorphism-everywhere; gradient text; cyan-on-dark neon-glow aesthetic. Everything that looks like it could have been made by AI with default prompts.
- **References**: Editorial design, long-form documentary storytelling, NWS/emergency-management visual language (for weather case), typographic posters.

### Design Principles
1. **Typography earns the hierarchy** — size contrast does more than decoration. A section that matters gets noticeably more typographic scale.
2. **No repeated card grids** — if three sections use the same 3-column card pattern, at least two of them need a different layout.
3. **Glassmorphism is a once-per-page device** — used at most for one key moment (hero metrics, reflection card). Not on every container.
4. **The liquid background is the atmosphere** — the animated orbs + grain texture are the distinctive environmental element. Content layers don't need extra decoration competing with it.
5. **Bans enforced**: No gradient text (ever), no border-left > 1px accent stripes (ever).
